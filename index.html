About

I love learning how people think and behave — and after enough years among my fellow humans, I'm comfortable saying I'm still learning.

In 2015 I took a sabbatical from work and transitioned into UX design with General Assembly. From then on, my view of service design changed: I learned to look past "nice" visuals and toward how people genuinely interact with a service or product, online and offline. My eyes and mind widened.

I continued to pursue UX by requesting a secondment to a team in my company, leveraging what I'd learned. My goal is simple — help people get things done in a seamless way (the process can always improve), and most importantly, make their lives a little better.

Case Study 01 · GIC

Redesigning the Intranet

StrategyDiscoveryAnalysisDesignProduction

GIC's intranet has always been the go-to place to empower and inform staff. As the company expanded locally and globally and the design aged, it needed to grow with the reach and needs of its people — so we rebuilt it around what staff genuinely care about.

From the project

What I did

  • Coordinated multiple stakeholder groups to understand how each sees the intranet and where it fits their plans.
  • Recruited and interviewed 30 staff by role to surface real needs and pain points.
  • Built personas spanning extreme users, on-the-go readers and content managers.
  • Ran 5 rounds of usability testing, iterating wireframes while keeping branding essential.
  • Prototyped a six-zone homepage: News, Upcoming events, Blogs & Yammer, Culture, Insights & GIC news, and Community outreach.

Impact

+2.3%Increase in viewership
−22%Bounce rate, 59% → 37%
+0.4mReading time, 1.8 → 2.2 min
Duration
~1.5 years
Teams
Technology, Corporate Communications, External Vendors

Case Study 02 · GIC Labs

Evaluating an AI Calendaring Tool

StrategyDiscoveryAnalysisDesignProduction

GIC Labs engaged us to explore whether technology — specifically AI — could improve daily workflow, with a focus on the perennial headache of scheduling meetings. The job was to find the truth, not to ship a tool for its own sake.

Affinity mapping · clustering trends & behaviours

In the room

What I did

  • Ran 5 user interviews and 2 field studies with personal and team assistants.
  • Mapped the current workflow and existing pain points around scheduling.
  • Used affinity mapping to pick up common trends and behaviours.

What we found

  • AI couldn't replace much of the human interaction the work actually relied on.
  • The existing systems didn't "speak" to one another — the real friction lived there.

Outcome

  • Recommended holding any AI implementation, backed by the findings.
  • Redirected the Technology team to fix underlying system bugs instead.
  • Shared practical scheduling tips observed during interviews with the users.
  • Helped the company avoid unnecessary cost.
Duration
~4 months
Teams
GIC Labs, Corporate Administration, Infrastructure Management
Case Study 03

Building an EV Charging Web App

StrategyDiscoveryAnalysisDesignProduction

In Singapore, EV private-hire drivers face fragmented charging — charge point operators run on separate networks, making it inconvenient to locate and use chargers across the island. We consolidated those operators into a single network for an efficient, reliable charging experience.

From the project

What I did

  • Recruited 8 PHV drivers and spent ~900 minutes in conversation with them.
  • Built personas spanning new EV drivers, two-year EV veterans and fleet companies.
  • Mapped an end-to-end journey: search → route → en-route → charging → malfunction → departure.
  • Ran 2 rounds of usability testing on wireframes, keeping branding essential throughout.

What drivers valued

  • Cheap parking and overall convenience.
  • Food amenities near charge points and access to DC chargers.
  • A place to share knowledge about EV charging.

Impact

+10%Increase in satisfaction rate
+15%Growth in active users
Duration
~1 year
Teams
Engineers, UI Designers, Product Managers
