About me

Learning humans is a lifelong project.

I love learning how people think and behave. Though not trained psychologically, the number of years I've spent on Earth and interacting with fellow humans has taught me one thing: humans are complicated — and that's exactly what keeps me learning.

2015 was the year I took a leap of faith, going on sabbatical to study UX design with General Assembly. From then, my perspective on service design changed. I learnt to see things not just as "nice" design, but how people actually interact with a service or product — online and offline.

My goal: building experiences that make work, communication, and technology feel a little more seamless and a lot more human.

Empathy plays a huge role in me and my eyes and mind began to widen.
